# Demo

## Concept explainer video

**File:** [`SHRUTI-concept-explainer.mp4`](SHRUTI-concept-explainer.mp4) — about 3 minutes, 854 × 480, no audio, ~1.3 MB.

An animated walkthrough of the SHRUTI concept:

1. Two pure tones (f₁, f₂) enter the ear through two separate transducers
2. A healthy cochlea generates a third tone at 2f₁ − f₂
3. What the microphone measures (spectrum, averaging, SNR)
4. The PASS / REFER decision
5. Why two separate transducers are used
6. What is inside the device (probe, preamp, codec, MCU, clock, power) and the PCB

> **Note:** this is a *concept animation*, not footage of a working prototype. Some scenes contain placeholder boxes where photographs or renders are still to be added, and the spectra shown are illustrative.
